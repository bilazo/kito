## Variants

`pulse` (default) fades the placeholder in and out. `shimmer` sweeps a
highlight from left to right — good for larger areas where pulse reads as
"flat".

Both respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: users who opt out of animation see
a static placeholder.

## Accessibility

`Skeleton` is `aria-hidden` — it's decorative and should not be announced.
Mark the region it fills with `aria-busy="true"` while data is loading so
assistive tech knows content is on its way:

```tsx
<div aria-busy="true">
  <Skeleton className="h-4 w-1/2" />
  <Skeleton className="h-4 w-full" />
</div>
```

The pulse animation respects `prefers-reduced-motion` — users who opt out
of animation see a static placeholder.

<ComponentPreview name="skeleton/busy-region" />

## RTL

Skeleton itself is direction-agnostic — it has no text, no icons, and no
directional layout of its own. Inside an RTL page it inherits the reading
direction of its container, so `w-2/3` or `w-1/3` size relative to the same
container edge as in LTR. The shimmer sweep is also direction-agnostic
(`90deg` gradient with a translateX keyframe), so it looks identical in
both reading directions.
