Wrap the app in a `TooltipProvider` **once** — typically in the root layout
— so all tooltips share the same hover delay. A `Tooltip` renders nothing on
its own; it wires a `TooltipTrigger` to its `TooltipContent`.

Icon-only triggers must still carry `aria-label` — a tooltip is not a
substitute for an accessible name.

## Sides

`side` accepts `top`, `right`, `bottom`, and `left`. The default is `top`.
Sides are **physical** — `left` / `right` do not flip in RTL. In an app
that serves both LTR and RTL, pick the side from the current direction
rather than a logical alias.

## RTL

`side` is physical — `left` / `right` do not flip in RTL. In an app that
serves both LTR and RTL, choose the side from the current direction:

```tsx
<TooltipContent side={dir === 'rtl' ? 'right' : 'left'}>
```

The tooltip content itself follows `dir="rtl"` on its subtree (via the
Provider or `<html dir="rtl">` in a real app), so Kbd and any inline text
inside stay correct.
