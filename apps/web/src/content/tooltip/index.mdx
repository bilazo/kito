Wrap the app in a `TooltipProvider` **once** — typically in the root layout
— so all tooltips share the same hover delay. A `Tooltip` renders nothing on
its own; it wires a `TooltipTrigger` to its `TooltipContent`.

Icon-only triggers must still carry `aria-label` — a tooltip is not a
substitute for an accessible name.

## Sides

`side` accepts `top`, `right`, `bottom`, `left`, `inline-start`, and
`inline-end`. The default is `top`.

## Long content

The tooltip wraps at `max-w-xs`. Combine `side` and `align` to keep it clear
of other UI.

<ComponentPreview name="tooltip/long-content" />

## RTL

Use `inline-start` and `inline-end` to place the tooltip on the reading
start or end. The tooltip flips with the page direction automatically.
